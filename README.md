# COIT20252---Business-Process-Management

## Introduction 

Business process management is an approach that organisations adopt to discover, analyse, design, improve and control 
business processes. Process management enables organisations to gain insight into their way of doing things and help 
improve their efficiency and performance. Business process management involves people, technology and methodology.
Effective process management enables organisations to cope with changes and align their activities with their 
strategic business objectives.


## e-portfolio 1 - Process Analysis

### Artefact 1 - SOPA: Sustainability-Oriented Process Analysis and Re-Design 

**https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10257-024-00695-x**

The article presents the SOPA approach for environmental sustainability business process analysis and re-engineering.
It integrates Life Cycle Assessment, activity based costing and business process simulation to enable the analysis of
the environmental footprint of specific activities and to compare the environmental impact of different process design.
The research also illustrates the framework by means of an example of a case study demonstrating how the process 
analysis can take into account other factors besides time and cost.

I chose this artefact as it expanded my understanding of process analysis. I mainly thought of process analysis as a
means to find delays costs and bottlenecks. The article demonstrates that organisations can also do environmental 
impact and sustainability analysis. It has value as an example of my BPM learning because it shows that process 
improvement decision can be made on more than one dimension of performance and shows that simulation can be used to
aid comparison of alternative prior to implementing process improvement decision.
